# Governance

Lakeloom is **maintainer-led**. Today there is a single maintainer, Elcio Abrahão
([@elcioabrahao](https://github.com/elcioabrahao)), who has final say on scope, design and releases.

- Anyone may propose changes through issues and pull requests (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- Significant design decisions are recorded as ADRs in `docs/decisions/`.
- Contributors who show sustained, high-quality involvement may be invited as maintainers.
  This document will be revised when the maintainer group grows.
- Conduct is governed by the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
